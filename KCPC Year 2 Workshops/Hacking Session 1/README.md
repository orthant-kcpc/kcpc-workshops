# Hacking Session 1 -- UKIEPC Problems

**Date:** 21 October 2026

Our first Year 2 hacking session takes place after UKIEPC. We will revisit problems from UKIEPC 2026, work through them together and discuss approaches.

Bring any UKIEPC problems you would like to solve or discuss, including problems you got stuck on during the contest. Participants are also welcome to bring other problems.
