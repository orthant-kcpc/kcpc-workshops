# Week 2 Workshop

**Important**: This workshop was hosted in collaboration with *KCL Tech* in our partnership with them in 2025 - 2026.

## Topic

The **topic** of this workshop is: ***Techniques for Investigation***.

Key themes covered:
- **Psychological Strategies & Spatial Thinking:** Challenging hidden 2D assumptions and utilizing 3D space diagonals (Pat's Dilemma, Polyhedra Dihedral Angles).
- **Wishful Thinking & Simplification:** Parameter specialization in functional recurrences and strong mathematical induction (The Long Sequence, Popcount Function).
- **Visual Proofs & Recasting:** Proofs without words (Sum of Cubes) and grid parity invariants (Mutilated Chessboard).
- **Viewpoint Changes & Symmetry:** Rotating reference frames (Clock Hands), Heron's reflection principle (Grandma's Cabin), and partitioning symmetry in expectations (First Ace).
- **The Extreme Principle:** Geometric contradictions via minimal distance (Sylvester-Gallai dual theorem).
- **Competitive Programming Applications:** Greedy Fibonacci bases / Zeckendorf decompositions (ARC 122 C) and XOR parity invariants (ARC 131 C).
